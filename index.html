Before we talk

A little clarity.

What should I include in my enquiry?

Your preferred area, budget in AED, property type and timing. For a sale, include the community and basic property details. Please leave out identity documents and bank details.

Are properties listed on this website?

This is a service introduction. Community examples are for inspiration; request a current shortlist and confirm availability, prices and fees before proceeding.

Can you arrange a mortgage?

Ask about an introduction to a licensed mortgage specialist. Eligibility, rates and approval depend on the lender and your circumstances.
